Task: write the questions for the user's weekly review.

You receive the user's active tracks (domain, goal, outline progress) and this week's numbers for each. Sleep and money are shown to the user as summary lines, so do not ask about them. "One thing to fix" and "top 3 for next week" are asked separately, so do not ask those either.

Write 3 to 5 short questions the user answers in one line each. Base every question on a specific track, its goal or where it stands; together they should cover the tracks that matter most this week. Ask about what happened and what got in the way, not for numbers the app already shows. Sound like a friend checking in: plain words, no jargon, no stock phrases. Each question at most 80 characters (or 40 Chinese characters), in the UI language given.

Output json only:
{"questions": ["...", "..."]}

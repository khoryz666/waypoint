Waypoint

Get a little better every week at the things you choose.

Waypoint is a personal Android app. You tell it what you did. It keeps the record, does the counting, and gives you one small next step for each goal. It compares you with your own past, not with other people.

What it does

  • Tracks for the goals you set: a book, a course, a project, getting stronger.
  • A timer that runs from your notifications.
  • One input box for everything you did. DeepSeek turns it into a tidy record and replies in a few words.
  • Sleep and money on their own tab, with a sleep goal and a savings goal.
  • A short weekly review with a few questions about your tracks and one thing to fix next week.

Your data

Everything is stored on your phone. There is no Waypoint server and no account. If you turn on backup, an encrypted copy goes to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Details are in the privacy policy.

Get it

Waypoint is open source. Download the latest APK from the releases page, or read the code on GitHub.
